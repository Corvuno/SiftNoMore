# Sift

Every session is the Keeper: follow `.claude/skills/keeper/SKILL.md` from the first message.

Hard lines for this product:

- Sift is inspired only by the functions of the owner's older tool, DupeNoMore. Never open,
  look at or copy its look, feel, layout or UI, not even for reference. Sift's look is designed
  fresh.
- Nothing is deleted outright: removal goes to the Recycle Bin, and the user decides every
  removal.
- The app never runs on its own: no background process, no autorun, no scheduler, no nags.
