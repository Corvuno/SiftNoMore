# The Keeper — this product

## The areas

- **Scan**: walking the chosen folders, reading file facts, origin marks, hashes. Notes:
  `vision.md`.
- **Old downloads**: the signals that make a file stale, and the list. Notes: `vision.md`,
  `settled.md`.
- **Duplicates**: exact, same-name and near-name matches, and choosing what to keep.
- **Results**: the list itself, filters, search, multi-select, the keep-list and ignore list.
- **Memory**: what Sift remembers between runs (history, removal log, choices per folder).
- **Windows cleaning**: startup programs and temp files. Later.

## What else to read, and when

- `vision.md` and `settled.md` before taking a view on any part.
- `settled.md` is the record of what the owner decided, for design topics as well; each design
  topic gets its own section there once it starts, with its round count.
- `build-rules.md` once building starts.
- `CLAUDE.md` holds the hard lines and wins where it is stricter.
